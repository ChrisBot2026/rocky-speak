How it works: Each character is sung as a chord of two pure tones — one from a low set, one from a high set (the same trick touch-tone phones used, tuned for phone speakers). Every word also gets its own whale-register drone chord with vibrato — that's the voice of Rocky — but the drones live below the signal band and never confuse the decoder. Word breaks are silences, decoded back into spaces by their length. The listener's mic runs a live FFT, watches the 16 signal frequencies, and votes each chord into a character. No server, no ML, pure math.
